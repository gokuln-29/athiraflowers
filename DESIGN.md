# Design: Athira flowers

Art direction set once in Phase 0. Tokens live in `src/app/globals.css`; this file is the reasoning behind them.

## The idea

Corporate and event floristry is about arrival: the arrangement is on the reception desk before nine, the table is dressed before the first guest. The site is built like a **florist's order docket and specimen board**: calm, exact, photographed close. Each product is a specimen (large photograph, a serif name, one plain price), and every commercial detail a corporate buyer needs (cut-off, delivery window, deposit, VAT, minimum) is stated in plain sentences next to it rather than hidden.

One memorable thing: **big, quiet photography with a serif that carries the personality**. Everything else (nav, filters, forms) is disciplined and recedes.

## Mode

Storefront = Persuade/Operate hybrid: browsing is Persuade (photography, type), checkout and account are Operate (clarity, consistency). Admin = Operate only (`interface-design`), with its own dense token set in `src/app/admin.css`.

## Palette (warm neutral, one accent)

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--background` | `#F1EDE7` | Page ground (warm greige, slightly cooler than cream) |
| `--surface` | `#F8F6F2` | Raised panels, product tiles |
| `--surface-deep` | `#E6E0D8` | Tone-step panels (replaces rules and borders) |
| `--foreground` | `#1E1A18` | Ink (full-strength running text) |
| `--muted-foreground` | `#5A524C` | Secondary text (6.4:1 on background) |
| `--primary` | `#8A2B3F` | Pomegranate: the only accent. Actions, links, price emphasis. 7.9:1 on white |
| `--primary-hover` | `#6F2132` | |
| `--night` | `#25201D` | One dark section per page (footer, corporate band); text `#EDE7DF` |
| `--success/warn/danger` | `#2F6B46` / `#8F6410` / `#B3261E` | Status only, always with text or icon |

Rules: one ground, one ink, one accent. Surfaces separate by tone step, not by lines or shadows. No gradients except a neutral scrim under text on photographs. Accent never exceeds ~5% of a screen.

Deliberately avoided: cream plus terracotta, violet, green-and-pink "garden centre", gold-on-black "luxury".

## Typography

- Display (Latin): **Newsreader** (optical-size serif, 400-500, tight tracking at large sizes, leading 1.02-1.1).
- Text/UI (Latin): **Instrument Sans** 400/500/600, 16px base, leading 1.55.
- Arabic display: **Amiri** (classical serif, matches Newsreader's contrast). Arabic text/UI: **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**. Arabic sizes get +6% and leading 1.7 for legibility.
- Scale (fluid, clamp): display 44-96px, h1 36-64, h2 28-44, h3 22-28, body 16-18, small 14. Five sizes visible on any page at most.
- No tracked uppercase labels, no monospace outside code, no styled accent word inside headlines, no numbered eyebrows. Prices use tabular numerals.

## Layout

- One grid: 12 columns, 20px gutters (mobile 16px outer margin, desktop 40-64px), content max 1360px. Large photography runs to the edge of the grid.
- Asymmetry inside the grid: headline left, photograph right, offsets by columns; never a centred stack of three icon cards.
- Section rhythm: 96-160px between sections on desktop, 56-72px on mobile. Neighbouring sections never share a composition (full-bleed photo, split with large type, product row, tone-step panel, dark band).
- Corners: 2px on photographs and buttons (square, editorial). Inputs 2px. No pill badges above headlines.
- Direction-neutral: logical properties only (`ms-*`, `ps-*`, `start`, `end`, `inline-*`). Directional icons flip with `rtl:-scale-x-100`.

## Motion

- Tokens: `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1)`, `--ease-in-out: cubic-bezier(0.77,0,0.175,1)`; durations 120ms (press), 200ms (hover/popover), 320-480ms (sheet), 700ms (hero entrance).
- One orchestrated moment: the home hero (headline lines clip-reveal, photograph settles from scale 1.04). Everything else is feedback to actions: button press `scale(.97)`, cart sheet slide, image hover zoom limited to 1.03, filter chips, quantity steppers.
- Transform and opacity only. Hover effects behind `@media (hover:hover)`. Everything honours `prefers-reduced-motion` (opacity only).
- No scroll-triggered fade-up on every section.

## Photography

Real photographs (Unsplash licence, credited in `docs/CREDITS.md` and the seed). Close, natural light, arrangements in real rooms where possible (reception desks, boardrooms, tables). Same crop ratios per slot: product 4:5, hero 3:2 on desktop and 4:5 on mobile, blog 16:10. Responsive WebP variants generated by the Images pipeline; `alt` text is bilingual and managed in admin.

## Voice

Plain, specific, confident. "Order before 2pm for same-day delivery in Dubai." Not "Elevate your space with bespoke blooms". Errors say what happened and what to do. Arabic copy is written for Arabic, not translated word for word (to be reviewed by a native speaker before launch; tracked in the launch checklist).

## Components (shadcn/ui, Base UI)

Button (primary solid accent, secondary tone-step, ghost), Input/Select/Textarea in `Field` groups, Sheet (cart, mobile nav), Dialog, Accordion (FAQ), Tabs, Badge (status only), Empty, Skeleton, Sonner/toast. Customised through tokens and `cva` variants, never per-use class overrides of colour.

## Admin (summary)

English only, LTR, dense: 14px base, cool-warm neutral, sidebar 248px, tables with sticky headers, slide-over editors, status badges with text, charts in the accent plus two neutrals. See `src/app/admin.css` and the interface-design notes in `docs/ADMIN_DESIGN.md`.

## Checks before a phase is called done

`web-design-guidelines` review over the changed UI; Playwright at 375 and 1440 in `en` and `ar`; console clean; no horizontal scroll; keyboard path works.
